01 / About

The long way
around is useful.

I have never thought of data and media as conflicting disciplines. Data uncovers the facts, patterns, and operational realities of a situation. Media is the vehicle for communicating those findings so people can act on them.

The foundation

Math gives me
the structure.

I completed a Bachelor of Science in Mathematics specializing in Statistics and Data Science alongside a Bachelor of Journalism in Broadcasting specializing in Media Production at the University of Nebraska-Lincoln, supported by a minor in Informatics.

The combination is deliberate. I like the exactness of a well-modeled problem and the human work required to make that problem understandable. Good analysis finds the shape of reality; good communication makes that shape useful.

Competitive rigor

Pressure is a
good teacher.

FBLA Collegiate gave me a practical arena to test ideas against strong peers and hard time limits. At the 2023 State Leadership Conference in Kearney, I placed first in Business Communications, Foundations of Finance, Information Management, Microeconomics, and Retail Management, with third place in Programming Concepts.

In 2022, I earned top-ten national finishes in Chicago for Administrative Support Technology, Computer Concepts, and Statistical Analysis after placing first in Administrative Support Technology and second in Statistical Analysis at state.

Campus impact

Leadership is
an operating system.

As Director of Operations for Campus NightLife, I led logistics, safety, volunteers, vendors, and post-event analysis for large-scale evening programs serving thousands of students. I focused on mentoring volunteers and using attendee feedback to make each program better than the last.

Within the Alpha Psi chapter of Delta Sigma Phi, I served as Secretary, President, and Vice President of Recruitment. I managed finances, enforced risk policies, expanded recruitment, and represented the organization to university and national leadership.

At Kamp Kaleo in Burwell, I counseled youth through outdoor challenges. That work built patience, active listening, and the ability to resolve interpersonal conflict when the conditions are unpredictable.

Working philosophy

01

Candor over corporate buzzwords

Polite honesty beats vague agreeableness. Identifying a problem directly is the only way to solve it efficiently.

02

Eliminate friction at the root

If a team is wasting hours on re-entry, fix the workflow, write the script, and document the standard.

03

Stay calm when the red light turns on

Whether it is a broadcast glitch or a broken pipeline, diagnose the failure point methodically and adapt.
